Exit enclosures (3.4.4.1)

The exit separation takes the floor-assembly rating required by 3.2.2, but not less than 45 min and not more than 2 h.

Why this is not a big table

Designers often expect a corridor-rating matrix by occupancy group. Part 3 does not work that way: Article 3.3.1.4 sets a single 45-minute default for public corridors, then relaxes it for sprinklered storeys — except where the corridor serves care, care and treatment, detention or residential occupancies. The full rule set, including the Sentence (4) no-separation reliefs and the electrically-supervised-sprinkler condition for Group E and F Div. 1/2 corridors, is in the repository data file.
